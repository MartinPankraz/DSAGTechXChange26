Welcome to your **red teaming hands-on lab** experience! This repos gets you all setup to embark on your assigned epic quest. Excited yet?

```text
Defenders often think in check lists to protect their SAP systems. Attackers think in graphs to exploit them. 
```

Today you will be switching sides🦹🏻‍♂️.

## Introduction

Anyone with a very basic understanding of SAP systems and security can participate in this workshop. The workshop is designed to be self-paced and can be completed in 2 hours. The workshop is divided into multiple quests, each quest will have a set of tasks that you need to complete.

This workshop gives you a glimpse into the world of a [red team'er](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_team). You will learn how to exploit hectic SAP BTP developers, their organizations, and how to detect & respond to ever evolving attacks.
